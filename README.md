Analyze the Superstore Sales dataset to understand business performance through sales and profit analysis.

## Approach

- Performed profit analysis.
- Created visualizations.
- Generated business insights.

## Results
- Monthly revenue trends were identified.
- Top-selling products were analyzed.
- Profit performance was evaluated.
- Business insights were obtained for decision-making.
